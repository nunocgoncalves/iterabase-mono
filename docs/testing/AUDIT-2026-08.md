## Decision and boundary

This audit applies the founder-approved ownership boundary from the *Atomic Platform Delivery and Pre-Production Confidence* engineering plan:

- **Control-plane product** owns portable API, identity, work, artifact, AgentPool, dispatch, harness, tool-gateway/tool-runner, and browser behavior.
- **Inference-gateway product** owns component-local inference routing, authentication enforcement, rate limiting, configuration, metrics, and request handling; only its producer/consumer seams are shared contracts.
- **Chart rollout** owns rendered resources and declarative install, upgrade, feature-enable, reapply, rollback, TLS, Service/EndpointSlice, persistence, selected-image composition, and component rollout behavior.
- **Forge/substrate** owns SSH/k3s bootstrap, reality-as-state, idempotent Forge reconciliation, overlay/Flux/secret handoff, host migration, and CPU/GPU substrate behavior.
- **Shared contract** owns a cross-process contract whose result depends on both named producers/consumers. The implementation remains close to those components; “shared” is not permission to centralize it in Forge.
- **External deployment overlay** owns customer/deployment-specific values and their source validation in its separate repository; accepted evidence may be referenced here but is not monorepo authority.
- **Production-only** owns only evidence that cannot be made representative without OPO1's real GPU, DNS/ACME, SMTP, firewall, customer storage, or resource constraints.

An assertion has exactly one authoritative owner below. A lower layer may retain a health or composition check, but it is marked **smoke** and must not be counted as duplicate authority. Deployment overlays are separate repositories; this audit uses Linear/accepted OPO1 evidence and does not copy overlay behavior into this monorepo.

Upgrades the supported predecessor to current, reapplies identical current intent, and requires unchanged pod UIDs for every Deployment, StatefulSet, and DaemonSet in both Helm releases, plus unchanged generated Secret digests, PVC/PV identities, and persisted markers. ### `observability`

- requires PostgreSQL and Redis targets discovered/up;

### `observability-tls`

- verifies issued Prometheus, Alertmanager, Grafana, and Loki leaves against the generated internal CA and exact Service DNS identities;

- includes chart fixes for datasource names/trust, sidecar CA/retry behavior, self-monitor schemes, Loki gateway trust, Promtail trust, and Grafana's localhost reload SAN.

### `internal-tls`

Requires the minimal platform's concrete identities, verified control-plane HTTPS, healthy gateway dependency clients, `verify-full`/CA-backed configuration, rejected authenticated plaintext Redis/PostgreSQL connections, and successful authenticated CA/DNS-verified TLS clients. It replaces the former Forge scenario's insecure Redis positive proof.
